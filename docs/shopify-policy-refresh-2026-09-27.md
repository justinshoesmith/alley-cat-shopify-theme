# Shopify policy refresh (27 September 2026)

The written policies below were edited in Shopify Admin. Unlike theme changes, they took effect on the published storefront immediately. Theme CSS changes remain on the unpublished `redesign/brand-refresh` draft until that theme is published.

| Surface | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refund policy introduction | Mentioned in-store and online returns; used two em dashes | Describes online purchases and eligible change-of-mind requests only, with no em dashes; retains the Australian Consumer Law statement |
| Change of Mind | Separate in-store and online purchase sections | Removed the in-store section and redundant online subheading; retained the online 14-day terms |
| How to Return Something | Directed customers to the retired Return Prime portal, then offered the contact form as an alternative | Contact form is the sole route, followed by the existing approval and posting instructions |
| Processing Times | Included an in-store exchanges sentence | Removed that sentence |
| Fault-notification wording | Required notice within 7 days of receiving the order and presented photos as mandatory | Requests that customers contact us promptly after discovering an issue, with order details or other proof of purchase and photos if available; no fixed notice deadline |
| Shipping policy | Linked to the old Shopify FAQs page | Links directly to the Chatwoot Shipping & Tracking category |
| Refund policy horizontal rules | Browser/theme-default rules | Draft theme styles policy dividers with the brand's subtle `--act-line` token and consistent spacing |

The live refund policy was rechecked after saving: no in-store wording, Return Prime URL, or em dashes remain; the contact form link and all six section dividers remain. The shipping policy URL was also rechecked. The other written policy bodies (privacy, terms, shipping) contain no horizontal rules or references to Gorgias, Reamaze, Return Prime, pickup, or the former street address.

This was a requested content and presentation update, not a legal review. The 14-day window still applies only to voluntary change-of-mind returns. The remaining refund policy terms, particularly statements about shipping costs and excluded change-of-mind categories, should be reviewed by a qualified Australian consumer-law adviser before further substantive changes.
